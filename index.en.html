Hungarian version

This profile hosts the official open-source projects, educational materials, and research and development initiatives of the Csindra Foundation. Our goal is to promote research in the natural sciences, health sciences, and engineering, as well as space exploration, robotics, and digitalization.

The Foundation’s official website: https://csindra.hu

The Foundation’s Activities

According to our charter, we focus on the following areas:

  • Curriculum Development: developing and coordinating educational and training materials for people aged 6–80 in multiple languages.
  • Scientific work: supporting the production and international dissemination of publications.
  • Robotics and CNC: supporting robot development, as well as research in robotics and automation.
  • Computer Science: modern development of information and communication technologies.
  • Healthcare: supporting research that aids medical and healthcare interventions.
  • Space Research: promoting scientific projects related to space research.
  • Talent Development: identifying, supporting, and mentoring talented young people.
  • Research Groups: establishing and continuously operating our own research groups.

Our open-source projects by age group

We manage our projects—which are thematically related but tailored to different age groups in terms of format and methodology—in three main repositories:

MERI

Hungarian Universities’ School of Robotics

Target age group: Ages 6–22

Focus: developing foundational educational and training materials, introducing younger generations to the world of robotics and computer science.

CAD

Computer-Aided Design

Target age group: Ages 16–32

Focus: computer-aided design, robotics, and the integration of AI (artificial intelligence), as well as curriculum development for university students based on Arduino and Raspberry Pi.

CNC

Computer Numerical Control

Target age group: Ages 26–80

Focus: robotics, repair, and automation research. Adult education and industrial applications to support space exploration and medicine.

How can you contribute?

Our community is open to all developers, educators, and researchers. You can help in the following ways:

  1. Bug reports and ideas: open an “Issue” if you find a bug or have a new idea.
  2. Code and documentation: submit fixes and contribute to existing learning materials (Pull Request).
  3. Testing: Test the written code and procedures on real hardware.
  4. Support: Help other members solve any problems that arise.

We reward the most active contributors with public recognition, certificates, and medals based on the Foundation’s values (BATKA, TALENTUM, KARÁT)!

Security and Ethics

Please always adhere to the Foundation’s privacy policy and code of ethics while working together. You can find the detailed license terms for each project in the respective repositories.
