CLIENT INFORMATION NOTICE
Regarding the Processing of Personal Data

In accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we provide the following information to our Users:

 

  1. 1.As the processing of certain personal data is strictly necessary for the provision of educational services, the consent of Users to such processing shall be deemed granted.  

  2. 2.Purpose of data processing: Identification, communication, provision of services, and mandatory data processing. 

  3. 3.Legal basis for data processing: Performance of a contract (engagement for electronic education) / Compliance with a legal obligation. 

  4. 4.Personal data may only be processed by the designated data controller(s) (including accountants and internet service providers), members of the foundation’s board of trustees, secretaries, and system administrators.  

  5. 5.Data shall only be transferred with the consent of the data subject. Such consent shall be deemed granted where the transfer is strictly necessary for the provision of education (e.g., cooperation with the management of a partner institution). 

  6. 6.Data subjects may access their own personal data at any time and request information regarding their progress and the safeguards in place. 

  7. 7.Data subjects may request the suspension of the processing of their personal data at any time; however, this does not apply in the event of official/regulatory or legal proceedings  

  8. 8.You may request the modification, restriction of processing, or deletion of your data at any time. However, such a request does not affect the Foundation’s obligation to retain data as required by law. 

  9. 9.Please note that restricting the processing of your personal data or withdrawing your consent may hinder the issuance of certificates or attestations. 

  10. 10.Should you believe your rights have been infringed, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority:
